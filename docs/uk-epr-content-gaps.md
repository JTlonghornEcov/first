# UK EPR: information pages we should produce

Found while linking the UK EPR explainer (v2). Each topic below is mentioned or implied on the UK EPR page, but the site
has no evergreen page whose main focus is that topic: today we either link to a service page, a dated news article, or
nothing. When a page is published, swap the "current link" on the UK EPR page for it.

## Priority 1: core explainers (high search demand, linked from the UK EPR page)

| Page | What it should cover | Current link used instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **What is a PRN (and a PERN)?** | What PRNs/PERNs are, how obligations are calculated, who buys them, why prices move, how PRNs sit alongside EPR fees | PRN audit offer page (`/request-free-prn-audit`) |
| **EPR fees explained** | Waste management fees vs PRN costs, how fees are calculated per material, Year 1 and later years, invoices and timing; ideal home for an interactive fee estimator | News article "Year 1 fees confirmed" |
| **Who counts as a producer?** | Producer types (brand owner, importer, packer/filler, distributor, seller, online marketplace, service provider), one-item-one-producer rules, worked examples | News article "UK EPR framework (Part 1)" |
| **Household vs non-household packaging** | What counts as household, why it drives fees, grey areas (e.g. on-trade, B2B), evidence to keep | Nothing |
| **UK EPR key dates and timeline** | Evergreen, regularly updated timeline: reporting deadlines, fee years, modulation, DRS, labelling; interactive timeline | Nothing (the page has deadlines only) |

## Priority 2: deepen authority and support conversion

| Page | What it should cover | Current link used instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Packaging data: what you need to report** | Materials, weights, packaging classes, nation of sale, data quality and evidence; links to Data Collection | Data Collection service page / nation-of-sale article |
| **Modulated fees and RAM ratings** | Red/amber/green ratings, how they change fees, what to do now; explainer graphic | RAM page (good) + RAM article |
| **Who's who in UK EPR** | Scheme administrator, the four nation regulators, Defra, what each does and when you deal with them | Nothing |
| **Penalties and enforcement** | What happens if you miss deadlines or misreport, resubmissions, audits | Nothing |
| **UK Deposit Return Scheme (DRS)** | Evergreen DRS page: scope, producers vs retailers, timeline, interaction with EPR | Two dated news articles |

## Priority 3: nice to have

- **EPR glossary**: every acronym (EPR, PRN, PERN, RAM, DRS, PPT, nation of sale, obligated tonnage). Great for internal linking and search snippets.
- **Small vs large producer: what you must do**: a full comparison (reporting, fees, PRNs, deadlines); the Small Producer Compliance Scheme page is an offer page rather than an explainer.
- **Plastic Packaging Tax vs EPR**: the difference, and who is caught by both (a common question; the PPT page exists).
- **EPR for online sellers and marketplaces**.
