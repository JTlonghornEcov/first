# Blog imagery brief

Drafts of 16 blog posts have been reformatted with the page builder. Every place that needs an image currently shows the
shared "Image to come" placeholder (asset 10489, `zz-draft-placeholder-image-to-come.png`). This brief says what should go in
each slot. Replace the placeholder in the draft, then publish the draft once it has been reviewed.

## Image specs

| Slot | Where it shows | Size to supply | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Header (hero)** | Right of the title at the top of the post | 1472 × 1012 px, landscape | Also used for the listing card and social sharing when no separate featured image is set |
| **Featured** | Blog listing cards and link previews | 1920 × 1080 px, landscape | Only needed where marked below |
| **Card** (image + text sections) | Beside a section, alternating left and right | At least 1200 × 1200 px | **Cropped to roughly square.** Keep the subject central and avoid text baked into the image, as it gets cut off |

Type key: **Photo** = stock or own photography. **Headshot** = portrait of a named person.
**Graphic** = simple branded illustration (map, timeline, figures). Graphics can be produced in-house from the post's own facts,
in the site's slate, yellow and white palette.
