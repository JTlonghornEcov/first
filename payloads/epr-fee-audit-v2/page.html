What does the EPR Fee Audit include?

Two things, delivered together. First, we audit your packaging data line by line against what you should be declaring. Then we act on what we find: correcting the declaration and advising on the pack changes that lower your fees from here on.

Part 1 · Audit

  • 01

    Data audit

    Supplier weights, material classification, primary/secondary/shipment splits, exports and B2B deductions, checked against our database of 4m+ SKUs.

  • 02

    RAM and modulation review

    The RAG rating of each pack under the Recyclability Assessment Methodology, and what sitting in the wrong band costs you.

Part 2 · Reduction

  • 03

    Fee correction

    A corrected obligation, with the evidence behind every change, ready for submission.

  • 04

    Reduction plan

    The specific pack changes (material, weight, format) that would move packaging into a lower fee band, ranked by saving.

Where do producers usually overpay?

In four places, and almost always in the data rather than the rules:

  1. 1Supplier default weights used instead of measured weights.
  2. 2Materials misclassified, for example a laminate booked in the wrong polymer band.
  3. 3Packaging exported or sold B2B that was never deducted from the obligation.
  4. 4Primary, secondary and shipment packaging split wrongly across the return.
YR 1YR 2YR 3YR 4

Every tonne over-declared is paid for again every year, so a few percentage points of error is a recurring cost, not a one-off.

How is this different from a compliance scheme?

A compliance scheme takes on your obligation and discharges it. We don't. Ecoveritas works alongside whichever scheme or direct registration you use, and makes sure the number you hand them is right.

That independence matters for this service in particular. Ecoveritas holds no PRN book, no waste contracts and no compliance scheme, so we have no commercial interest in the size of your obligation. We earn from the number being right and from it coming down.
