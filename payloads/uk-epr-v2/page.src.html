The basics

What is Extended Producer Responsibility?

EPR for packaging is new UK legislation that requires producers to take responsibility for and pay the total cost of dealing with their packaging from when it is placed onto the market to the end of its life.

If you are obligated under EPR, you are responsible for collecting and reporting data for all your obligated packaging placed onto the UK market.

Builds on the Packaging Waste Regulations, in place since 1997: the regime behind PRNs

The new regulations build on the existing Packaging Waste Regulations, and it is hoped that their implementation will reduce the environmental impact of packaging.

Am I a small producer or a large producer?

The line can be fine, but it makes a big difference! Depending on whether you are small or large affects the actions you are required to take, so it's important to know where you sit, and what steps you need to take once you are seated!

Small producerSmall Producer Compliance Scheme →
Who
Annual turnover of £1m–£2m and handle over 25 tonnes of packaging, or turnover over £2m but handle 25 to 50 tonnes of packaging.
Report by
On or before 1 April each year, starting in 2025 (for packaging handled in 2024).
Large producer
Who
Annual turnover of over £2m and handle more than 50 tonnes of packaging.
Report by
On or before 1 October each year, for 1 January to 30 June of that year; and on or before 1 April each year, for 1 July to 31 December of the previous year.
