How it works

Four draws, and you don't have to top the leaderboard to win any of them.

  1. Be active, earn points

    Like, share and post about Ecoveritas on LinkedIn. Every action scores, and your total builds across the whole round.

  2. Every point is a raffle ticket

    Score at least one point in a month and you're in that month's £100 draw. Every point is another entry, so the more you do the better your odds.

  3. Your points count twice

    Every point also becomes a ticket in the £225 grand draw at the end of the round. Nothing resets — October points are still in the hat in December.

Scoring

Points are for engaging with that week's posts — the ones going out now, while they're still live. The two three-pointers are capped at one each per week, so steady beats sporadic.

    Repost any of that week's Ecoveritas posts to your own network.

  • 1 per week3pts

    Original Ecoveritas-related post

    Your own organic post, or a share of a related company's post with your own take — EPR news, packaging regulation, life at Ecoveritas, client wins. You must tag the Ecoveritas page: no tag, no points.

Weekly caps

One scoring share-with-comment and one original post per week, resetting every Monday

Likes and plain shares are uncapped — engage with everything we publish. The two three-pointers score once each per week, so a single thoughtful share and a single original post is all it takes to max them out.

Post more than that and it's still very welcome, it just doesn't add points that week. Save it for Monday and it does.

Why: a steady drumbeat across the quarter makes us look alive. A pile of posts in the last week doesn't.

How the draws work

Both draws are raffles, and your points are your tickets. Everyone who scores gets a fair shot, and the people who put more in hold more entries. Every point you earn goes into two hats at once: that month's £100 draw, and the £225 grand draw in December.

  1. 1 — Score

    Earn one point or more during the month and you're automatically entered. No sign-up, no form.

  2. 2 — Collect tickets

    Every point is one entry in that month's draw and one in the grand draw. Score 20 points, you're in both hats 20 times.

  3. 3 — The draws

    One name wins £100 at the end of each month. Then on 18 December one more name is drawn from everyone's full-round totals for £225.

One thing before you post

Everything you share carries the Ecoveritas name, so please hold it to the standard we’d apply to any client-facing work. We win on our own merits, not at anyone else’s expense — so no digs at competitors, named or implied, and nothing about a client, scheme or regulator we wouldn’t be comfortable defending publicly. Confident about what we do, never disparaging about anyone else.

If you’re unsure how something reads, the brand book is the place to check — it covers our tone of voice, how we talk about regulation, and the values behind it. And if in doubt, send a draft to Marketing. We’d far rather look at something first than unpick it later.

Ground rules

Kept simple on purpose — this should feel like a nudge, not admin.

  • One action, one score. Each company post can earn you a like and a share once. A share with your own take replaces the plain share rather than stacking on top of it.

  • That week's posts. Likes and shares score on posts published during the current week. Digging through the archive doesn’t add points.

  • Comments must be genuine. “Great post!” doesn’t score. Add a real thought in your own words — that’s what makes a post travel.

  • Tag the page and you’re done. Tagged posts are picked up automatically — no tracker, no forms, nothing to log. Forget the tag and the post is invisible to scoring.

  • Employees only. Only Ecoveritas people can score points and enter the draws. Likes from clients and contacts are very welcome, they just don’t count towards the leaderboard.

  • Stay on brand. Professional, accurate, and never at a competitor’s expense — see the note above. No confidential client information, ever.

  • Weeks run Monday to Sunday. The weekly caps reset Monday morning, so a post on Sunday and another on Monday both score. Scoring runs automatically each Monday and the leaderboard goes out the same day.

  • The round runs 1 October to 18 December. Monthly draws take place in the first week of the following month. The grand draw is made when the round closes, with the prize paid before Christmas. Both draws are made at random from the tickets held, and are witnessed by someone outside Marketing.
