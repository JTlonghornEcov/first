We support Close Austria

Austria

Packaging must be licensed with an approved collection and recovery system, and household and commercial packaging are handled separately. Austria introduced a deposit on single-use drinks bottles and cans in 2025.

Key law
Packaging Ordinance 2014 (VVO), under the Waste Management Act
Register
EDM register (Federal Ministry)
How producers comply
Licence with a collection system, e.g. ARA

Foreign distance sellers need an authorised representative in Austria.

We support Close Belgium

Belgium

Belgium runs one packaging regime across its three regions, overseen by a single interregional commission. Household and industrial packaging are covered by separate schemes.

Key law
Interregional Cooperation Agreement on packaging (2008)
Register
Interregional Packaging Commission (IVCIE)
How producers comply
Fost Plus (household) and Valipac (industrial)

Companies placing more than 300 kg of packaging a year must report.

We support Close Bulgaria

Bulgaria

Producers either join a licensed recovery organisation or pay product fees themselves. Registration is with the Executive Environment Agency. Bulgaria is also planning a deposit return scheme and has released a draft act.

Key law
Waste Management Act and Packaging Ordinance
Register
Executive Environment Agency (ExEA)
How producers comply
Licensed recovery organisation, e.g. Ecopack

Producers outside a collective scheme pay product fees to the environmental fund (PUDOOS).

We support Close Croatia

Croatia

Croatia's packaging system is run centrally by the national Environmental Protection and Energy Efficiency Fund, which collects fees and reports. A digital producer register replaced paper filings in 2025.

Key law
Ordinance on packaging and single-use plastic products (2023)
Register
RPPO register (FZOEU)
How producers comply
Register and pay fees to the Fund

Single-use drinks containers also carry a refund (deposit) fee.

Most producers transfer their take-back and recovery obligations to an authorised packaging company and report annually.

Key law
Packaging Act No. 545/2020
Register
Ministry of the Environment
How producers comply
Authorised packaging company, e.g. EKO-KOM

Producers without an establishment in Czechia need an authorised representative.

We support Close Denmark

Denmark

Denmark's packaging EPR has been in operation since 1 October 2025. Producers register, report household and commercial packaging separately, and most comply through a producer responsibility organisation. Drinks bottles and cans are also covered by a long-running deposit return scheme (Dansk Retursystem).

Key law
Executive Order No. 1146 of 2025 on packaging
Register
Dansk Producentansvar (DPA)
How producers comply
Join one of the approved PROs

Fees on reports from October 2025 are environmentally graded.

Finland

Packers and importers above the turnover threshold must join a producer organisation that runs collection and recycling.

Key law
Waste Act and Government Decree on packaging
Register
Pirkanmaa ELY Centre producer register
How producers comply
Producer organisation, e.g. Rinki

Drinks containers are covered by the separate Palpa deposit system.

We support Close France

France

Producers must join an approved eco-organisation and obtain a unique identification number, which must appear on their terms of sale. Consumer packaging needs sorting information. EPR for professional (commercial and industrial) packaging is due to start in 2027.

Key law
Environmental Code, as amended by the AGEC law (2020)
Register
ADEME producer register (UID number)
How producers comply
Eco-organisation, e.g. Citeo, Léko or Adelphe

Triman logo and Info-tri sorting labels apply to household packaging.

We support Close Germany

Germany

Every producer of packaging that ends up with private final consumers, or comparable sources of waste generation such as restaurants, hotels and offices, must register in LUCID and license it with a dual system before sale.

Key law
Packaging Act (VerpackG)
Register
LUCID (Central Agency Packaging Register, ZSVR)
How producers comply
System participation with a dual system

Online marketplaces must check that their sellers are registered.

We support Close Hungary

Hungary

Since July 2023 a state concession operator, MOHU, runs packaging waste management. Producers register and pay EPR fees quarterly. MOHU also runs Hungary's deposit return scheme for drinks bottles and cans, in place since January 2024.

Key law
Government Decree 80/2023 on EPR
Register
MOHU and the OKIRkapu portal
How producers comply
Quarterly declarations and fees to MOHU

The separate environmental product charge is handled by the tax authority (NAV).

Key law
Waste Management (Packaging) Regulations 2014
Register

How producers comply
Compliance scheme membership (Repak)

A deposit return scheme (Re-turn) has run for drinks containers since 2024.

Registration and contribution to Afvalfonds

We support Close Poland

Poland

Producers register in the BDO database, report annually and meet recovery targets, usually through a recovery organisation. A wider EPR reform is in progress. Poland's deposit return scheme for drinks containers started in October 2025, and the government has released a draft act that would repeal certain existing measures.

Key law
Act on Packaging and Packaging Waste Management (2013)
Register
BDO database
How producers comply
Recovery organisation, or pay the product fee

Reform proposals would move to a state-led model, with full EPR planned from 2028.

Producers transfer their responsibility to a licensed managing entity and declare the packaging they place on the market. A deposit return scheme for drinks containers was implemented in April 2026.

Key law
Decree-Law 152-D/2017 (UNILEX)
Register
Portuguese Environment Agency (APA)
How producers comply
Managing entity, e.g. Sociedade Ponto Verde

Registration and reporting run through APA's SILiAmb platform.

Register

Approved PRO
