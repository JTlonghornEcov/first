Australia

Mandatory
Scheme and authority
The Australian Packaging Covenant, administered by the Australian Packaging Covenant Organisation (APCO), under the National Environment Protection (Used Packaging Materials) Measure. E-waste is regulated separately under the National Television and Computer Recycling Scheme.
In scope
Packaging brand owners and importers under the Covenant, and importers or manufacturers of computers and televisions under the e-waste scheme.
How we help
We manage your Covenant membership and reporting, keep you aligned with state specific rules, and are already tracking the upcoming Recycling and Waste Reduction Act so you're ready when it lands.
