The challenge

Different rules in every market. One clear plan.

Each country sets its own rules, fees and reporting for packaging, WEEE, batteries and textiles, and they keep changing. Without a map, compliance becomes slow, expensive and risky.

Where teams get stuck
  • Working out which regulations apply in each market
  • Knowing what to do first, and what can wait
  • Finding the time and specialist knowledge to research it all

How it works

Four steps, from your business to a roadmap your team can act on.

  1. Understand your business

    We learn about your operations, products and supply chain to see where obligations may arise, and agree your priorities.

  2. Map your obligations

    Our regulation database covers over 80 countries. We identify every rule relevant to you: packaging, WEEE, batteries and textiles.

  3. Build your overview

    A country-by-country matrix of what applies, what you must do and what's changing, turned into a phased plan.

  4. Review and refine

    We walk your team through the findings in a close-out meeting and agree the next steps together.

What you'll receive

Clear outputs you can act on straight away, and the option to keep them up to date.
