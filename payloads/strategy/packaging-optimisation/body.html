Common questions

Will this compromise product protection?

No. Optimisation is about the right level of packaging, not simply less of it. Protection is part of the assessment throughout.

How does this relate to RAM scoring?

Recyclability is assessed alongside cost and protection, so changes tend to improve your RAM ratings, and lower your modulated EPR fees, as a natural result.

Is this a one-off review or an ongoing service?

Either. A single review to reset a pack design, or ongoing checks as materials, formats and regulation change.
