Selling into the EU from the UK, the US or elsewhere? You already need an authorised representative.

Since 12 August 2026, producers not established in the EU must appoint one in each Member State where they make packaging available (Article 45(3)). We start this in parallel with your assessment.

The regulation

What PPWR asks of you

  • Proof that each pack meets the rules. A technical file and a Declaration of Conformity, signed by you, for every distinct pack.
  • Evidence from your suppliers. Recycled content, substances of concern, minimisation and marking, each backed by documents.
  • Someone accountable inside the EU. An authorised representative, if you're established outside it.
  • A plan for what comes next. Harmonised labelling, then the 2030 design rules.

We take all of it off your desk, from the first question to a declaration ready to sign, and keep it current when your packaging changes.

Before you book

Does PPWR apply to a UK or US business selling into the EU?

Yes, if you make packaging or packaged goods available on the EU market. Since 12 August 2026 you also need an authorised representative in each Member State where you sell.

Do I need a Declaration of Conformity for every SKU?

No. A declaration is issued per distinct pack. SKUs that share components, materials and weights share one declaration, and it's valid in every EU country.

Who signs the declarations?

You do: responsibility for compliance doesn't transfer. We prepare each declaration and check the evidence supports it before it reaches you.

Are scheme, registration and representative fees included?

No. You pay those directly to the relevant body, so they never sit inside our invoice. We estimate them for you in the assessment.

Can we contract only part of the service?

Yes. The assessment can be contracted on its own, and it's the best first step because it sets the scope and cost of everything after it.
