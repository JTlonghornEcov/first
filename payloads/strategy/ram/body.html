Who it applies to

Large producers of household packaging, one component at a time.

Only large producers who supply household packaging are obliged to assess it using the RAM, and every individual packaging component must be assessed separately.

The rating decides your fee. Since 2026, assessed packaging attracts varying EPR fees, known as modulated fees: red packaging attracts the highest costs and green the lowest.

Why it matters

Red costs more. Every year it stays red.

A pack rated red pays the highest fee for as long as it's on the market, so the rating is worth getting right, and worth improving.

What does the output mean?

The RAM gives each kind of packaging a rating of red, amber or green.

Red

Packaging with elements that make it difficult to recycle at scale.

Highest fee
Amber

Some difficulties in being widely recycled, such as limited collection and sortation, needing specialist infrastructure to reprocess, or an output limited in use or quality.

Middle fee
Green

Widely recyclable in the current UK infrastructure.

Lowest fee

What is involved in the RAM?

Five stages of assessment, for every component.

  1. Classifying

    What material type is used? Which packaging unit are you assessing?

  2. Collection

    How widely is the material collected by local authorities?

  3. Sorting

    Can the packaging be sorted at scale?

  4. Reprocessing

    Can the packaging be reprocessed easily and without harm?

  5. Application

    Are there any contaminants that would affect the process or output?
