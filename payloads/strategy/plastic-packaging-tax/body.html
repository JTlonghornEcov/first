Who pays

UK manufacturers and importers of plastic packaging.

If you make or import 10 tonnes or more of finished plastic packaging in 12 months, you must register with HMRC and file a return every quarter. Tax is due on every component with less than 30% recycled plastic.

Where several UK businesses work on the same packaging, the one that makes the last substantial modification before it's filled is liable. For imports, it's the business the packaging is brought in for.

The rate

£228.82 per tonne

From 1 April 2026. The rate rises every April, so every tonne you can't evidence as 30% recycled costs a little more each year.

The essentials

  • Quarterly returns

    Due, with any tax, by the last working day of the month after each quarter. Nil returns are still required.

  • Six years of records

    Keep them for every accounting period, and keep records below the threshold too, in case you cross it.

  • Some packaging is exempt

    Including immediate packaging for licensed human medicines and transport packaging for imported goods.
