Ecohub

Hand it over. We do the rest.

Most EPR tools still expect your team to upload the data and chase your suppliers for it. Ours doesn't. You send us your sales or purchase data, and our team does everything else in Ecohub, our secure EPR data platform: from your suppliers to an audit-ready return.

  • Your suppliers deal with us, not you. We contact them directly and collect their packaging data.
  • Real weights, wherever you are. We measure packaging at our own facility or on site at yours, in the UK or abroad.
  • Gaps filled without your help. Missing data comes from verified sample weights in our database of 4m+ SKUs, so coverage stays at 100%.
  • Checked before it's filed. Every dataset is cross-checked against previous periods, and anomalies are investigated.

Ecoview

See what your packaging is costing you, and what to change.

Ecoview turns your packaging data into answers for three things: your EPR bill, your compliance and your sustainability. It's an interactive analytics platform built in Power BI, designed for everyday users, not BI experts.

  • Reduce your bill. See which packs and materials drive your EPR fees, and where a redesign or a better rating would bring them down.
  • Stay compliant. Spot complex, poorly rated or incomplete packaging at SKU and component level before it reaches a return.
  • Choose better suppliers. Compare suppliers on materials, recyclability and data quality, so you know who is helping and who is costing you.
  • Become more sustainable. Track recycled content and red, amber and green ratings over time, and estimate your Plastic Packaging Tax exposure by supplier.
