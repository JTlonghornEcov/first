PPWR has applied across the EU since 12 August 2026. Packaging placed on the EU market needs technical documentation and a Declaration of Conformity.

The problem

Every customer is asking you the same questions

Under the EU Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR), the brands you supply can't keep selling into Europe without evidence that their packaging complies. That evidence starts with you.

So the questionnaires arrive. Each customer has its own format, its own deadline and its own follow-ups, and many are waiting on your answers before they can move. Some are holding off on any other compliance work until you send them a declaration.

That puts you at the centre of the value chain. It also means answering the same questions again and again.

FAQs

Questions suppliers ask

Is the PPWR Declared service really free?

Yes. We produce your Declaration of Conformity at no cost. In return, we ask you to tell us which of your customers sell into the EU, so we can handle their documentation requests directly.

Who is responsible for the accuracy of a self-declared DoC?

You are. A PPWR Declared badge reflects information you have provided and confirmed. If you'd like independent evidence that your packaging meets the requirements, PPWR Lab Verified gives you that.

What does lab validation involve?

We agree which packaging formats to sample, send them to an independent laboratory, and compare the results with the data you've given us. If the results confirm your data, you receive the PPWR Lab Verified badge. If they don't, we tell you what needs attention.

How much does lab validation cost?

It depends on how many packaging formats need testing. Tell us what you supply and we'll give you a clear quote.

My customers have already asked me for data. Can I still use this?

Yes. Send us what you've already gathered and we'll build on it. From then on, you can direct every new request to us.

Will you share my data with my customers?
