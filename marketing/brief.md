# Email marketing brief

Status: **draft**. Sections marked *proposed* are my starting position, not agreed. Update as we agree things.

## Business
Ecoveritas (ecoveritas.com): UK B2B packaging compliance. Packaging EPR (pEPR) reporting and fees, PRNs/PERNs,
packaging data audits.

## Goals (proposed)
1. Pipeline: book qualified calls / quote requests for pEPR and data-audit services from obligated producers.
2. Retention and expansion: keep existing clients informed and on top of deadlines; cross-sell (e.g. PRN clients to data audit).
3. Authority: be the source UK packaging, sustainability and finance teams trust on regulatory change.

Primary metric: clicks and booked calls attributable to email, not opens (Apple MPP inflates opens).

## Account facts (audit 2026-10-01)
One audience, 1,394 subscribed. Sends so far reach at most 232 (mostly the ~183 "current clients" tag).
Big untapped groups: Information Request 523, Survey 176, webinar registrants. Of the 757 contacts with a known location, 311 (41%) are in the UK.

## Audiences (proposed, to confirm against the audit)
- Clients, by service (pEPR, PRN, data audit).
- Prospects: obligated producers (large / small), by role (sustainability, packaging tech, finance, ops).
- Partners / intermediaries, if any.
- Lapsed / unengaged: no click in 12 months.

## Voice (proposed)
Plain-English expert. Lead with the deadline, cost or risk, then what to do. Specific numbers and dates over adjectives.
UK spelling. No scare tactics, no greenwashing.
