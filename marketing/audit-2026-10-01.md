# Mailchimp audit: 1 October 2026

Read-only audit of the Ecoveritas Mailchimp account (server us12), run with `npm run mc:audit`. It covers the
last 24 months (Oct 2024 to Sep 2026). Aggregate data is in `audit/summary-2026-10-01.json`. Raw data is in
`audit/raw-2026-10-01.json`, which is gitignored.

**About the benchmarks:** they're my estimates for UK/EU B2B professional services email, drawn from published
industry studies, not from your data. Apple Mail Privacy Protection (MPP) and corporate link scanners inflate opens,
so this report puts more weight on clicks.

| Metric | Typical B2B prof. services (estimate) |
|---|---|
| Open rate (MPP-inflated) | 30–40% |
| Click rate (unique clickers / delivered) | 2–4% |
| Click-to-open | 8–15% |
| Unsubscribe rate | 0.1–0.3% |
| Hard bounce | < 0.5% |
| Total bounce | < 2% |

## Summary

**The content works, but it reaches very few of your contacts.** Your audience has 1,394 subscribed contacts, yet no
campaign in 24 months went to more than 232 of them. Most sends go to the same ~183 "current clients" contacts. When
people do get an email they click at roughly 3–5x the benchmark. But about 1,000 contacts (mostly website enquiries,
survey respondents and webinar sign-ups) never hear from you. There's no welcome or nurture sequence, the sending
domain isn't authenticated, and sending stops for months at a time.

## Account and audience

- **One audience** ("Ecoveritas", created March 2024), so there's no overlap between lists to check.
  - Status: 1,394 subscribed, 7 unsubscribed, 46 cleaned, 0 pending.
- **Growth:** about 480 subscribed contacts in Jan 2025, 801 in Aug 2025, 1,392 in Sep 2026, so the list nearly tripled in 20 months.
  - Spikes come from webinars: +137 in Aug 2025 (survey) and +254 in Aug 2026 (EU PPWR webinar with Veolia).
  - New contacts are still arriving at about 1–3 a day through the website form.
- **Where subscribed contacts came from:** 849 imports (61%), 517 website embed form (37%), 24 added by hand, 4 via the API.
- **Joined by year:** 446 in 2024, 436 in 2025, 512 in 2026.
- **Engagement health:** 929 of 1,394 subscribed contacts (67%) have never opened, and 1,160 (83%) have never clicked.
  - This is mostly because they've never been emailed, not because they've lost interest.
  - Mailchimp's 5-star contact rating is 1–2 stars for every contact, so it's no use for segmenting yet.
- **Location:** unknown for 637 contacts, because Mailchimp works out location from opens.
  - Where it's known: GB 311, US 103, then Brazil, India, Germany, the Netherlands, Cambodia and South Africa (17–33 each).
  - The EU PPWR webinars are drawing an international audience that UK pEPR content won't suit.
- **Segmentation is done entirely with static tags.** There are 18 tags, including:
  - "Information Request" (523), "EU PPWR Webinar 2026-08-19" (232), "current clients" (209), "Survey" (176)
  - "NPWD large filtered out" (156), "Attended" (152) and "Did Not Attend" (80)
  - "Prospect" (79), "Horticulture" (78) and "HTA Webinar Attendees" (78)
  - Two tags are empty.
  - "current clients" was last updated in May 2024.
  - There are no interest groups and no saved conditional segments.
- **Merge fields (13):** they include Company, Title, Type, Status, Enquiry, Site Location and "How did you first hear about us?".
  - Birthday is public on the form, which is odd for B2B. "Full Name" duplicates First and Last Name.
  - Fields have generic tags (MMERGE6–13). I haven't checked how many records have Type or Status filled in.
- **Sign-up and consent:** there's one sign-up form, and double opt-in and GDPR marketing-permission fields are both off.
  - The permission reminder says *"You are receiving this email because you opted in via our website"*, which is untrue for the 849 imported contacts.
- **Account timezone is set to America/New_York.** Scheduling and reports use US Eastern time.
- **Industry isn't set on the account,** so Mailchimp's industry benchmarks aren't available.

## Deliverability basics

- **The sending domain ecoveritas.com is verified but not authenticated** (no Mailchimp DKIM signing).
- **Client sends get persistent soft bounces:** about 9 of ~183 recipients on almost every send (4.9–7.3%).
  - That pattern looks like corporate mail filters rejecting the mail, not addresses that no longer exist. This is likely, not proven.
- **13 hard bounces (7.1%) on the 12 Aug 2026 client send.** The client contact data is going stale.
- **Three different sender identities:** "Ecoveritas" (info@), "Ecoveritas" (a staff address) and "James".
  - The default sender name for the audience is "James".
- The footer has a postal address and the archive bar is on. The consent wording is covered under Account and audience above.

## Top recommendations (ranked by impact vs effort)

| # | Recommendation | Effort | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Authenticate the sending domain, fix the timezone and use one sender identity | Low (DNS changes, about 1 hour) | High |
| 2 | Start emailing the ~1,000 contacts who are never mailed, introduced properly and segmented | Medium | Highest |
| 3 | A monthly calendar, with webinars as the engine and a reusable webinar sequence | Medium | High |
| 4 | A welcome and nurture journey for new sign-ups | Medium | High |
| 5 | Clean up client and contact data, and build a proper segmentation model | Low–Medium | Medium, and it supports 2–4 |

1. **Authenticate the domain (DKIM, plus SPF/DMARC alignment).** Set the account timezone to Europe/London and pick one sender.
   - Why: about 5% of every client send soft-bounces. Gmail, Yahoo and Microsoft now expect authenticated bulk mail.
   - Every later recommendation depends on reaching the inbox. It should also lift delivered numbers straight away.
2. **Start emailing the ~1,000 contacts you're not reaching** (Information Request 523, Survey 176, webinar registrants and the remaining imports).
   - Introduce yourselves properly first: say what they'll get and make opting out easy.
   - Then split them: UK pEPR or global/EU PPWR, and prospect or client.
   - The numbers: you have 1,394 contacts but send to at most 232. Even at half your current 11% click rate (my estimate), that's about 50 extra engaged prospects per send.
   - Do this after #1, and roll out in batches, most engaged first, to protect sender reputation.
   - Before sending, I need you to confirm where the 849 imported contacts came from and on what legal basis you can email them.
3. **A monthly calendar, with webinars as the engine.**
   - Webinar emails are your best performers (21–34% click rate against 4–8% for generic updates). There were no sends at all in two of the last eight quarters.
   - One reusable sequence: invite → reminder → last chance → recap with slides and recording → Q&A page → sales follow-up to people who clicked.
   - In between, a short monthly regulatory update tied to pEPR and PPWR deadlines.
   - Host the resources on ecoveritas.com, built with our Craft scripts, rather than as PDFs or Drive files.
4. **A welcome and nurture journey.**
   - About 57 sign-ups a month in 2026 currently get nothing. The welcome email has been paused since October 2024.
   - A 4–5 email sequence over 3 weeks: welcome, the pEPR obligations explainer, a case study or proof, the data-audit offer, and a call to book a call.
   - Build it as a draft. It only starts when you confirm.
5. **Clean up client and contact data.**
   - Tag hard-bounced client contacts so they're excluded, and ask account managers for replacement contacts. That's 13 lost in August, 7% of the client list.
   - Bring the "current clients" tag up to date (last refreshed May 2024) from the CRM or billing system.
   - Replace one-off tags with fields: relationship (client, prospect, partner), service, region (UK, EU, rest of world) and role.
   - Hide Birthday and drop the duplicate Full Name field.

**Lower priority:**
- Build one responsive branded template.
- Correct the permission reminder wording, and decide on double opt-in and GDPR fields for the form.
- Test Tuesday vs Thursday sends once volume allows.
- Archive old drafts, but only after you confirm.
