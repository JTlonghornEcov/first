Mandatory since 2021
Scheme and authority
Section 18 EPR Regulations under the National Environmental Management: Waste Act, overseen by the Department of Forestry, Fisheries and the Environment.
In scope
Paper, packaging, electricals, lighting, batteries and lubricant oils.

Kenya
