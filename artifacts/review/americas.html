Markets we support across the Americas

United States

Active

Legislated state by state, not federally.

CaliforniaOregonColoradoMarylandMinnesota
Scheme and authority
No single federal scheme. Packaging EPR is currently law in California (CalRecycle), Oregon (Department of Environmental Quality), Colorado (CDPHE), Maryland (MDE) and Minnesota (MPCA), with further states expected to follow. Battery and e-waste rules are legislated separately, state by state.
In scope
Packaging placed on the market by brand owners, importers and, in some states, retailers. Several states also regulate batteries and e-waste separately.
How we help
We assess your obligations across every relevant state, register you with the right authorities and Producer Responsibility Organisations, and manage your ongoing reporting, so you deal with one team instead of five separate schemes.

Canada

Active

Run province by province.

Recycle BCÉco Entreprises QuébecCircular Materials
Scheme and authority
Producer-funded programmes including Recycle BC in British Columbia and Éco Entreprises Québec in Québec, with other provinces operating their own stewardship programmes or organisations such as Circular Materials.
In scope
Packaging producers, brand owners and importers, with several provinces also regulating electricals and batteries separately.
How we help
We register you with the right provincial programmes, manage your reporting calendar and keep you compliant as provinces update their rules, all from one team.
