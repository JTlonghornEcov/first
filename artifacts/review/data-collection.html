The challenge

The hard part isn't the rules. It's the data.

Turning scattered, incomplete packaging information into something accurate enough to submit, across markets with different rules and deadlines. One gap or missed date can mean fines or costly rework.

Independent

We don't trade PRNs or run compliance schemes. Our only job is your data.

So our advice is impartial: based on accurate data and what's best for your business.

Three stages, one team

From raw data to submitted reports, run end to end in Ecohub, our secure EPR data platform.

Stage 1

Data collection

  • Receive and structure. Your sales or purchase data, checked and imported into Ecohub.
  • Measure. Packaging weighed at our own facility or on site at yours, plus data from you and your suppliers.
  • Validate. Cross-checked against your history, with discrepancies investigated.
  • Fill the gaps. Verified sample weights from our database of 4m+ SKUs, so coverage stays at 100%.
Stage 2

Data calculation

  • Quality-check. Tonnages by material and department compared with previous periods.
  • Model. Our EPR calculation models turn validated data into accurate material weights.
  • Prepare outputs. Submission-ready data for each market, so you pay the correct fees.
Stage 3

Registration and reporting

  • Registrations. We guide your team or manage it for you, liaising with regulators directly.
  • Best-fit options. Independent insight on PRN markets and membership costs.
  • Many markets. Registrations across global EPR frameworks, without the admin.
  • 4m+SKUs in our packaging database
  • 100%Data coverage, with gaps filled from verified sample weights
  • 25+ yearsRunning packaging data for brands, retailers and manufacturers
