Where producers usually overpay

In four places, and almost always in the data rather than the rules.

  1. Supplier default weights used instead of measured weights.
  2. Materials misclassified, for example a laminate booked in the wrong polymer band.
  3. Packaging exported or sold B2B that was never deducted from the obligation.
  4. Primary, secondary and shipment packaging split wrongly across the return.

Every tonne over-declared is paid for again every year, so a few percentage points of error is a recurring cost, not a one-off.

What the audit includes

We audit your data line by line, then act on what we find.

  1. Data audit

    Supplier weights, material classification, packaging splits, exports and B2B deductions, checked against our database.

  2. RAM and modulation review

    The rating of each pack under the RAM, and what sitting in the wrong band costs you.

  3. Fee correction

    A corrected obligation, with the evidence behind every change, ready for submission.

  4. Reduction plan

    The pack changes that would move packaging into a lower fee band, ranked by saving.
