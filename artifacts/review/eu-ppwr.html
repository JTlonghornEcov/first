EU Packaging & Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR)

PPWR introduces new requirements covering packaging design, recyclability, labelling and documentation for packaging placed on the EU market.

It has applied since 12 August 2026, with further requirements rolling out through 2030 and beyond.

What PPWR is

Harmonised rules for packaging placed on the EU market.

PPWR replaces the previous Packaging Directive. As a regulation, it applies directly and in the same way in every member state.

  • Recyclability
  • Packaging design
  • Labelling
  • Reuse and refill systems
  • Substances of concern
  • Extended Producer Responsibility

Who does it apply to?

If your business places packaging on the EU market, PPWR may apply.

  • Manufacturers

    Produce packaging or commission packaging under their brand.

  • Importers

    Bring packaged goods into the EU market.

  • Brand owners

    Sell products under their own name or trademark.

  • Distributors and e-commerce sellers

    Place packaged goods on the EU market.

Key milestones

PPWR applies now, and its biggest design requirements land in 2030. Your packaging plans need dates attached.

  1. August 2026Applies nowPPWR applies

    PFAS banned in food contact packaging.

  2. August 2027Producer registers

    Member states establish EPR registers.

  3. August 2028Packaging labelling

    Harmonised material composition labels.

  4. August 2029Reusable QR codes

    Reusable packaging must include QR codes.

  5. January 2030Core requirements begin

    Recyclability grades, recycled content, reuse targets, SUP bans, packaging minimisation.

  6. January 2035Recyclability at scale

    Packaging must be recyclable within waste systems.

  7. January 2038Minimum recyclability standard

    Packaging must achieve at least 80% recyclability (Grade B).

EPR and PPWR

Two regimes, one programme

EPR funds recycling and is declared market by market. PPWR decides whether a pack can be sold in the EU at all. Different rules, same data: that is why we run them together.

Comparison of EPR and PPWR
TopicEPRPPWR
ScopeNational, one scheme per countryEuropean, one regulation
What it doesFunds collection and recyclingSets the rules a pack must meet to stay on the market
Unit of workThe declaration, on each market's calendarThe distinct pack
Multiplies by marketYesNo. One declaration is valid in every market
RegistrationThe producer registers in each marketNothing to register
