Packaging Optimisation

Your packaging drives material costs, shipping costs, recyclability scores and, increasingly, what you owe under EPR. We look at all of it together, so the changes you make actually move the needle.

Get in touchLearn about the RAM

Cost, shipping and fees, considered together.

We assess how your packaging performs in practice: through the supply chain, on shelf and at end of life. Then we find where it's over-engineered, under-protective, or simply costing more than it needs to.

A change that helps one cost and hurts another isn't really an improvement, so material cost, shipping cost and compliance fees are always weighed together.

Common questions

Will this compromise product protection?

No. Optimisation is about the right level of packaging, not simply less of it. Protection is part of the assessment throughout.

How does this relate to RAM scoring?

Recyclability is assessed alongside cost and protection, so changes tend to improve your RAM ratings, and lower your modulated EPR fees, as a natural result.

Is this a one-off review or an ongoing service?

Either. A single review to reset a pack design, or ongoing checks as materials, formats and regulation change.
